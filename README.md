# p–Pb Master Analysis

High-energy physics research project focused on the analysis of proton–lead collision data recorded by the CMS detector at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC).

Work conducted within the CMS Collaboration through the GASP-IF/UFRGS and SPRACE/UNESP research groups.

Development of C++ and ROOT macros to study the correlation between the mean transverse momentum ⟨p<sub>T</sub>⟩ and charged-particle multiplicity N<sub>ch</sub>, aiming at the extraction of the squared speed of sound c<sub>s</sub><sup>2</sup> in the produced system.

Important: This work uses data collected by the CMS experiment and contain results that are not published by the CMS Collaboration.
